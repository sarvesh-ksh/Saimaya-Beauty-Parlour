# Saimaya Makeup Parlour & Academy Website

A modern, premium, and fully responsive business website for **Saimaya Makeup Parlour & Academy**, designed to establish a strong online presence and showcase beauty services, bridal makeup expertise, and professional makeup training programs.

## About The Project

Saimaya Makeup Parlour & Academy is a beauty and makeup destination located in Sinnar, Maharashtra, offering professional beauty services and certified makeup training courses.

This website provides a clean and luxurious digital experience for potential clients and students while highlighting the academy's expertise, services, and achievements.
